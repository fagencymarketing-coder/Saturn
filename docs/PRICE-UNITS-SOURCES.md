## Task B: Волски Аминатор application rate

- **No official rate with units was found.** The official site https://www.volskybiochem.com has no Aminator page:
  - The product categories list Диформы, Моноформы, Страда, Микроэл, Экомак, Микромак and Спецпрепараты (Оптим, Эффект).
  - The site search for «аминатор» returns «найдено 0 элементов».
  - volskybiohim.ru / volsky-biochem.ru could not be reached through the proxy.
- Context found:
  - glavagronom.ru (June 2026) reports that РосАгрохимслужба is running **registration trials** in Нижегородская обл. of «жидкие органоминеральные удобрения производства компании «Волски Биохим»: «Аминатор» марки Молибден и «Аминатор» марки Молибден-Бор» on peas, including seed treatment. Source: https://glavagronom.ru/news/novye-agrohimikaty-dlya-goroha-i-yarovoy-pshenicy-ispytyvayut-v-nizhegorodskoy-oblasti
  - This means the product is probably **not yet in the Госкаталог**, so no official регламент exists yet.
  - Trademark «АМИНАТОР» is registered to ООО «Волски Биохим», dated 01.04.2026 (per the Google snippet of a trademark-registry page).
  - Do not confuse it with the registered «Аминатор» (Люкс/Альфа/Базис…, рег. № 834-11-4489-1) of **ООО «Торфопродукт»**. That is a different product. Source: https://www.agroxxi.ru/goshandbook/agrochem/834-11-4489-1.html
- Recommendation: do not put units on «0,5». Ask the dealer or Волски for the label. For comparison only, Волски's other foliar products are given in л/га (Страда N: 3–5 л/га) and the seed-treatment lines in л/т. «0,5» could be л/т (seed treatment, which is how it is being trialed on peas) or л/га. This is unconfirmed.
